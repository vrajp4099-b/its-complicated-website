Frequently asked questions

Answers about this academic project and the proposed learning support model.

Which programmes does this concept cover?

The website describes potential support for Cambridge and IGCSE learners. It is not an official school or affiliated provider.

Can I book an actual demo class?

No. This is an MBA college project demonstration. Form submissions illustrate an enquiry process, but no real classes or appointments are offered.

What happens after I submit a form?

The form sends your enquiry to the project team and emails a receipt to the address you enter. Demo dates are preferences only.

Are fees and schedules available?

No real course fees, tutors or class schedules have been established for this project.

Is this affiliated with Cambridge International?

No. This is an independent educational support concept and is not affiliated with Cambridge International.
